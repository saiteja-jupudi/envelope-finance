Savings plans & balances

For each savings envelope, enter what you had saved before SpendWise (one time). For a chit fund or RD, add the plan to see instalments left, the payout, and how its return compares with an FD.

How much should each envelope get?

Add your bank statements and SpendWise works out what you usually spend on rent, groceries, eating out and the rest, then suggests a monthly limit for each.

Your statements stay on your phone

Statements you add here are read and analysed only on this phone. They are never uploaded to SpendWise or anyone else, including anyone you share the budget with, and SpendWise does not store them. Signing out, closing the app or tapping “Clear statements” removes them.

What is saved: only the envelopes and limits you choose to save (and anyone you share the budget with can see them). Payee labels you pick (for example “Rent”) are kept on this phone only.
